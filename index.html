Backup and how recommendations work

Your ratings, imports, and explored artists are saved in this browser. They don’t sync between devices. Download a backup before clearing browser data.

Starter picks come from your four-week plan, not Spotify listening history. Live picks use the public iTunes catalog for song metadata only, then offer searches on YouTube, SoundCloud, and Bandcamp. Playback stays on those platforms.

Replay and skip ratings adjust artist and mood priorities locally. Imported artists become additional seeds. A daily rotation and refresh button vary the selection. This is a transparent rule-based recommender, not an AI or Spotify recommendation service.

Live results refresh when you visit after 24 hours or press Refresh picks. Availability depends on the catalog and your connection; unavailable results are clearly labeled. No account, secret API key, or subscription is required.
