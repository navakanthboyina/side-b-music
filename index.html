A little help finding your next favorite.

The catalog supplies real, unseen songs first. AI then selects from those songs using your individual likes and dislikes, including choices across artists. No subscription or API key. Lightweight AI uses a much smaller model, with roughly 280 MB of model weights and around 1 GB of GPU memory required. Allow extra browser storage for supporting files. It needs a compatible WebGPU device. Loading can take several minutes; use Wi-Fi. This smaller model may know fewer songs, especially regional or obscure tracks.

Your taste is processed locally. Model files come from external hosts; artist searches go to iTunes. Language and mood filters describe the catalog search direction, not verified audio features. AI ranks song metadata; it does not listen to audio. No playlist import is required: like a few songs to start. See your song feedback →

Backup and how recommendations work
Your ratings, imports, explored artists, and 14-day recommendation history are saved in this browser. They don’t sync between devices. Download a backup before clearing browser data.

      
Starter picks come from your two-week plan, not Spotify listening history. Live picks use the public iTunes catalog for song metadata only, then offer searches on YouTube, SoundCloud, and Bandcamp. Playback stays on those platforms.
Likes, dislikes and Already know apply to one song, never its whole artist. Older artist-level ratings are archived and ignored. Imported artists are optional seeds for Catalog mode only. Fresh picks exclude songs shown on this device during the previous 14 days. Refresh rotates through artists and remembers your history after reloads. If no unseen songs are found, it says so instead of repeating them. Your current saved batch can stay visible until you refresh; the two-week plan and comfort mixes remain fixed. AI mode runs lightweight Qwen2.5 locally through WebLLM to suggest specific songs and explain their fit to the songs you liked. Catalog mode uses rules without AI. Neither mode connects to Spotify recommendations.
Catalog results refresh when you visit after 24 hours or press Refresh picks. AI batches generate when you press Refresh picks; saved results remain on reopen. AI does not run while the page is closed. Availability depends on the catalog and your connection; unavailable results are clearly labeled. No account, secret API key, or subscription is required.
